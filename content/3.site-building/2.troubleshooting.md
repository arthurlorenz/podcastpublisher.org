---
title: Troubleshooting
description: Check missing forms, feed items, episode links, and analytics counts.
---

## I cannot find Podcasts or add an episode

Confirm that the main module is enabled. Check the role's [Podcast Publisher permissions](/site-building/permissions), especially **Access podcast overview page** and the relevant **Create** permission. The podcast list is `/admin/content/podcast`; the episode form is `/podcast-episode/add`.

## An episode is missing from the feed

Open `/podcast/{podcast_id}/feed.xml` with the correct numeric ID. Check **Published** on both the podcast and episode, and confirm that the episode belongs to that podcast. The default View sorts items by **Authored on** date; it does not sort by episode number.

## The feed item points to the homepage

Create and publish a [Podcast Episode Page](/get-started/episode-page) node linked to that episode. The feed item link falls back to the site homepage when no linked page exists.

## I cannot select an episode on its page form

The installed reference field may be hidden from the form. Add **Podcast Episode** to the content type's **Manage form display** as described in the [episode page guide](/get-started/episode-page).

## Audio upload fails

Check the allowed extensions on the episode form (`mp3`, `wav`, `aac`) and your site's upload limits, file permissions, and PHP configuration. The size shown on the form is environment-specific.

## Analytics still shows zero

Make sure the optional module is enabled and the feed View's **Podcast Episode: Audio File** field uses **Download Tracker URL**. Confirm the enclosure URL contains `/podcast-publisher/feed/`. Request the tracked URL with `GET`, allow Drupal cron to process its queue, and reload the report. HEAD requests, recognized bots, duplicate requests within 24 hours, and short partial requests may not increase the count. See [Analytics](/analytics) for the full setup.
