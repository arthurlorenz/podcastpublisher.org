Podcast Publisher supplies podcast and episode entities, a Podcast Episode Page content type, and a Views feed. Set up editor access before handing the workflow to a publishing team.

- [Permissions](/site-building/permissions) lists the module's roles and access checks.
- [Episode pages](/get-started/episode-page) explains how to add the fields your project needs, expose the episode selector, and create a page linked to an episode.
- [The feed](/get-started/feed) explains the public URL, default filtering, and the View you can customize.
- [Customize the feed](/site-building/feed) maps each feed tag to its View setting and explains episode GUIDs.
- [Fields and display](/site-building/fields-and-display) covers custom fields, the audio player on episode pages, and template overrides.
- [Analytics](/analytics) covers the optional alpha module and its required View formatter.
- [Update and uninstall](/site-building/updates) explains database updates and what uninstalling removes.
- [Troubleshooting](/site-building/troubleshooting) collects the most common checks.

The guides describe the 1.0.0-alpha10 release. Verify custom fields, displays, and Views settings against your own site's configuration.
