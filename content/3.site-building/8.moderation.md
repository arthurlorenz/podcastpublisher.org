---
title: Review drafts
description: Keep revisions of podcasts and episodes, and review changes before they reach the feed with Content Moderation.
---

Podcasts and episodes keep revisions, like nodes. The forms have a **Create new revision** checkbox and a **Revision log message**. A page to browse and revert revisions is not available yet. With Drupal core's **Content Moderation** module, editors can save drafts and a reviewer publishes them.

## Turn on moderation

1. Enable **Content Moderation** at **Extend**, or run `drush en content_moderation`.
2. Open **Configuration → Workflow → Workflows** (`/admin/config/workflow/workflows`) and edit a workflow, such as **Editorial**.
3. Under **This workflow applies to**, select **Podcast** and **Podcast Episode**, then save.
4. At **People → Permissions**, grant each role the workflow's transition permissions, such as **Editorial workflow: Use Create New Draft transition**. Grant **View latest version** to roles that review drafts.

The podcast and episode forms then have a **Change to** select with the workflow's states instead of the **Published** checkbox.

## How drafts behave

- A draft of a published podcast or episode stays out of the feed. The feed keeps the published version until the draft is published.
- The latest version is at `/podcast/{id}/latest` or `/podcast-episode/{id}/latest`, for users with **View latest version**.
- An unpublished podcast hides all its episodes from the feed, published or not.
- Users with **View unpublished podcast** or **Edit podcast** see unpublished podcasts; **View unpublished podcast episode** or **Edit podcast episode** do the same for episodes.

[Scheduled publishing](/site-building/scheduling) does not support moderated episodes yet. Use one or the other for episodes.

## When a user account is cancelled

Podcasts and episodes follow the method chosen when an account is cancelled:

| Cancellation method | Podcasts and episodes of the user |
| --- | --- |
| Disable the account and keep its content | Stay as they are. |
| Disable the account and unpublish its content | Are unpublished. |
| Delete the account and make its content belong to Anonymous | Belong to Anonymous, and so do the user's revisions. |
| Delete the account and its content | Are deleted. Deleting a podcast also deletes all its episodes, including other users' episodes. The user's revisions of other users' podcasts and episodes are deleted or, for current revisions, given to Anonymous. |

Check which podcasts a user owns before deleting the account with its content.
