---
title: Notify apps of new episodes
description: Tell WebSub hubs and the Podcast Index right away when a feed changes.
---

Podcast apps check feeds on their own schedule, so a new episode can take hours to appear. The optional Podcast Publisher Notify module announces each change as it happens. It needs a Podcast Publisher release newer than 1.0.0-alpha10.

## Turn on notifications

```bash
drush en podcast_publisher_notify
```

The module notifies two kinds of services:

- **WebSub hubs.** Apps and directories subscribe to a hub and fetch the feed when the hub passes on a notification. The default hub is Google's `https://pubsubhubbub.appspot.com/`.
- **The Podcast Index.** Apps that use its catalog learn about the update right away. No account is needed.

## Configure the services

Open **Configuration → Web services → Podcast feed notifications** (`/admin/config/services/podcast-publisher-notify`):

1. Enter one WebSub hub URL per line in **WebSub hubs**. Leave it empty to notify no hub.
2. Uncheck **Notify the Podcast Index** to skip it.
3. Click **Save configuration**.

The page needs the **Administer podcast settings** permission.

## When notifications are sent

A notification goes out when you save a published podcast, or publish, change, unpublish, or delete a published episode. It is sent after the page has been delivered, once per podcast, however many episodes changed. Episodes released by [Scheduler](https://www.drupal.org/project/scheduler) notify when cron publishes them.

Each feed also links to itself and to the configured hubs, as the WebSub standard requires:

```xml
<atom:link href="https://your-site.example/podcast/12/feed.xml" rel="self" type="application/rss+xml" />
<atom:link href="https://pubsubhubbub.appspot.com/" rel="hub" />
```

The `rel="self"` link is part of every feed, even without this module.

## Check that it works

Notifications are logged. Open **Reports → Recent log messages** (`/admin/reports/dblog`) and filter by the type `podcast_publisher_notify`. A message is logged for each notification sent and each one that failed.

- Hubs fetch the feed from its public URL. A local development site cannot be reached, so the hub ignores or rejects the notification.
- The notification contains the absolute feed URL. Cron run by Drush needs the site's URL, for example `drush --uri=https://your-site.example cron`. Without it, the notification names `http://default/podcast/12/feed.xml` as the feed.
