---
title: Check and share the feed
description: Find the RSS URL, inspect its contents, and share it with listeners.
---

Your RSS feed is the connection between your site and your listeners. It lives on your domain and lets podcast apps fetch episodes from your site. You can share it directly and submit it to the directories you choose.

Each podcast has an RSS feed at:

```text
https://your-site.example/podcast/{podcast_id}/feed.xml
```

Replace `{podcast_id}` with the podcast's numeric ID from **Podcasts** (`/admin/content/podcast`). For example, podcast `12` has `/podcast/12/feed.xml`.

## What the feed contains

- The podcast's title, summary, type, and other entered show metadata appear in the channel.
- Each published episode becomes an item with a title, description from shownotes, publication date, and an audio enclosure with URL, MIME type, and file length.
- Episodes are ordered by their **Authored on** date, newest first.
- The configured feed View filters out unpublished podcasts and unpublished episodes.
- A linked [Podcast Episode Page](/get-started/episode-page) supplies the item link. Without one, the feed falls back to the site homepage for that link.

The default feed display is part of Drupal's **Podcast Feed** View at `/admin/structure/views/view/podcast_feed`. A site builder can [customize it](/site-building/feed), so a site with edited View settings may differ from these defaults.

## Check it before sharing

Open **Podcasts → your podcast → Feed check** (`/admin/content/podcast/{podcast_id}/feed-check`). It checks what directories reject most often:

![The Feed check page listing errors for the artwork and missing podcast information, warnings, and passed checks](/images/feed-check.png)

*A podcast whose artwork is not square, without an iTunes category and owner email.*


- **Artwork:** present, square, JPG or PNG without transparency, 1400 to 3000 pixels.
- **Podcast information:** title, description, iTunes category, explicit flag and owner email.
- **Podcast GUID** and **Language**.
- **Episode enclosures:** every published episode has an audio file that exists, with a size and type.
- **Boolean values:** the feed outputs `true` and `false` as Apple requires.
- **Episode URLs:** click **Check episode URLs** to request each enclosure URL.

The page also links external validators, which test how apps parse the feed. Drupal's **Reports → Status report** lists podcasts whose feed check has problems.

Then check the feed itself:

1. Open the feed URL in a browser and confirm the podcast title and expected episode items are present.
2. Check each `<enclosure>` URL and confirm that the audio file is publicly reachable.
3. If you use analytics, confirm the enclosure URL contains `/podcast-publisher/feed/` after [configuring tracking](/analytics).
4. Copy the complete public feed URL for listeners or a podcast directory. Then [submit it to directories](/get-started/directories).

If an episode is missing, first check that **Published** is enabled on both the episode and its podcast. See [Troubleshooting](/site-building/troubleshooting) for other checks.
