---
title: Make an episode page
description: Link a Drupal content page to a published podcast episode.
---

Podcast Publisher installs a **Podcast Episode Page** content type with a **Podcast Episode** reference field. A page is optional, but it gives listeners a dedicated URL and gives the feed item a link to that page.

## Show the episode selector

On the checked-out alpha9 release, a fresh site's page form may omit the **Podcast Episode** field even though the field is installed. A site builder can expose it at **Structure → Content types → Podcast Episode Page → Manage form display** (`/admin/structure/types/manage/podcast_episode_page/form-display`). Move **Podcast Episode** into the form, choose an autocomplete widget, and save the display. Keep the field required.

## Create the page

1. Open **Content → Add content → Podcast Episode Page** (`/node/add/podcast_episode_page`).
2. Enter a title and choose the matching episode in **Podcast Episode**. The autocomplete selection includes the episode ID.
3. Leave **Published** checked when the page should be public, then save it.

![The Podcast Episode Page form with an episode reference selected](/images/episode-page.png)

*The Podcast Episode selector was enabled in the local site's form display before this screenshot.*

After a page is linked, the episode's feed item uses the page URL in its `<link>` element. Without one, the current alpha9 feed falls back to the site homepage. The audio enclosure still points to the episode's uploaded file or its [analytics tracking URL](/analytics).

You can customize the page's display and add other fields through Drupal's normal content type tools. Those changes do not replace the Podcast Episode entity or its audio file.
