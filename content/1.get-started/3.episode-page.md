---
title: Make an episode page
description: Add the fields your project needs, then create a page linked to an episode.
---

Podcast Publisher installs a **Podcast Episode Page** content type with a **Podcast Episode** reference field. A page is optional, but it gives listeners a dedicated URL and gives the feed item a link to that page.

## Prepare the content type

Before editors create pages, a site builder should add the fields the project needs. The installed content type is a starting point for your site's episode pages.

1. Open **Structure → Content types → Podcast Episode Page → Manage fields** (`/admin/structure/types/manage/podcast_episode_page/fields`).
2. Add fields for the content you want on the page, such as an introduction, a transcript, guest information, or an image. Choose these to suit your project; they are not required by Podcast Publisher.
3. Use **Manage form display** to arrange the fields for editors.
4. Use **Manage display** to choose how those fields appear to listeners.

Keep the installed **Podcast Episode** reference field: it connects the page to its episode. Audio and feed shownotes belong to the referenced episode; adding page fields does not automatically put their contents into the RSS feed.

## Show the episode selector

On the checked-out alpha9 release, a fresh site's page form may omit the **Podcast Episode** field even though the field is installed. A site builder can expose it at **Structure → Content types → Podcast Episode Page → Manage form display** (`/admin/structure/types/manage/podcast_episode_page/form-display`). Move **Podcast Episode** into the form, choose an autocomplete widget, and save the display. Keep the field required.

## Create the page

1. Open **Content → Add content → Podcast Episode Page** (`/node/add/podcast_episode_page`).
2. Enter a title and choose the matching episode in **Podcast Episode**. The autocomplete selection includes the episode ID.
3. Fill in the additional fields configured for your project.
4. Leave **Published** checked when the page should be public, then save it.

![The Podcast Episode Page form with an episode reference selected](/images/episode-page.png)

*This example shows the basic form with the Podcast Episode selector enabled. Your project's additional fields will also appear here.*

After a page is linked, the episode's feed item uses the page URL in its `<link>` element. Without one, the current alpha9 feed falls back to the site homepage. The audio enclosure still points to the episode's uploaded file or its [analytics tracking URL](/analytics).
