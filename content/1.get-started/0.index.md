Podcast Publisher lets you publish from your own Drupal site, with hosting you control and an RSS feed on your own domain. Your site holds your show details and episodes; listeners can subscribe through podcast apps that support RSS.

The module adds **Podcast** and **Podcast Episode** content entities to Drupal. It also installs a **Podcast Episode Page** content type for optional pages linked to episodes. It does not create a Media type.

## Install

The 1.0.0-alpha9 release declares support for Drupal 9, 10, and 11. Install it with Composer, then enable the main module through Drupal's **Extend** page or Drush. The `@alpha` flag lets Composer install the alpha release even when your project only accepts stable packages.

```bash
composer require 'drupal/podcast_publisher:^1.0@alpha'
drush en podcast_publisher
```

The module depends on Drupal's File, Image, Link, Node, Taxonomy, Text, User, and Views features. Composer and Drupal resolve the declared dependencies during installation.

The [analytics module](/analytics) is optional and has a separate setup step.

## Publish your first feed

1. [Create a podcast](/get-started/create-podcast) at **Podcasts → Add podcast**. Give it a title and choose **Episodical** or **Serial**.
2. [Add an episode](/get-started/add-episode) with an audio file. Choose the podcast and leave **Published** checked when it is ready for listeners.
3. Open `https://your-site.example/podcast/{podcast_id}/feed.xml`, replacing `{podcast_id}` with the podcast's numeric ID. The podcast list at `/admin/content/podcast` shows each ID.
4. Check the channel title and episode enclosure URL before you share the feed URL.

The feed includes only **published podcasts and published episodes**. Saving a podcast without an episode creates a feed with no episode items. The module generates a feed; it does not submit that feed to listening directories for you.

## Choose a path

- **Editors:** [Create a podcast](/get-started/create-podcast), [add an episode](/get-started/add-episode), and [make an episode page](/get-started/episode-page) if your site uses them.
- **Site builders:** [Assign permissions](/site-building/permissions), [check feed behavior](/get-started/feed), and [set up analytics](/analytics) if needed.

If an expected form or feed is missing, see [Troubleshooting](/site-building/troubleshooting).
