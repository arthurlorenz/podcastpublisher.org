---
title: Create a podcast
description: Set up the podcast details used by its RSS feed.
---

Go to **Podcasts → Add podcast** in Drupal, or open `/admin/content/podcast/add`. Your role needs **Create podcast** permission. A site administrator can [assign permissions](/site-building/permissions).

![The podcast form with title, subtitle, summary, a Media Library image, and tabs for the feed settings](/images/create-podcast.png)

*The required fields are Title, Type and Medium. The tabs appear with the Field Group module. The screenshot uses sample content from a local Drupal 11 site.*

## Enter the essentials

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | The podcast name. Required and used as the feed's channel title. |
| **Type** | **Episodic** or **Serial**. Required. |
| **Summary** | A short description of the show for the feed. |
| **Subtitle** | A brief supporting line for podcast apps. |
| **Image** | Show artwork, as a media item. Pick one from the Media Library or upload a new image, with useful alternative text. Directories require a square JPG or PNG of 1400 to 3000 pixels. |

The other form fields let you add a general **Category**, up to three predefined **iTunes Category** terms, a **Funding** link, **Copyright**, an owner **Email**, feed **Author** references, and the **Explicit** and **Complete** flags. **Authoring information for the feed** is separate from Drupal's internal author field.

Podcast apps that support the [Podcasting 2.0 tags](/site-building/feed#channel-tags) also show:

- **People**: the regular hosts and other people of the show. Type a name to pick a person, or a new name to add one. New episodes start with these people.
- **License** and **License URL**: the license of the audio and video, such as `CC-BY-4.0`. A custom license needs the URL of its full text.
- **Medium**: what the episodes are, such as **Podcast**, **Music** or **Audiobook**. **Podcast** is the default.

Leave **Published** checked when the podcast is ready to appear in its feed. You can save it unpublished while preparing the show.

With the [Field Group](https://www.drupal.org/project/field_group) module installed, the form groups the optional fields into tabs below the main fields, such as **Feed settings**, **People**, **Rights** and **Feed ownership and moves**.

Click **Save**. Drupal opens the podcast page. The podcast list at `/admin/content/podcast` shows its numeric ID and an **Episodes** link. With up to 10 podcasts, each also gets its own entry under **Podcast Publisher** in the admin menu, with **Edit** and **Episodes** links; sites with more podcasts use the list.

## Manage people

A person is entered once and reused on every podcast and episode. Edit their name, role, image and link at **Podcasts → People** (`/admin/content/podcast/people`). Changes show in every feed that lists them.

The role, such as **Host**, **Guest** or **Producer**, belongs to the person and applies wherever they appear. People added by typing a new name start as **Host**; change the role in the People list.

The People list needs **Edit podcast person**; adding and deleting there need **Create podcast person** and **Delete podcast person**. Anyone who can edit a podcast or episode can add people by typing a new name.
