---
title: Add an episode
description: Upload audio, write shownotes, and publish a podcast episode.
---

Open **Podcasts → your podcast → Episodes → Add podcast episode**. The episode form is also at `/podcast-episode/add`. Starting from a podcast's episode list selects that podcast and suggests the next episode number.

![The episode form with the podcast, label, uploaded audio file, a Media Library image and shownotes](/images/add-episode.png)

*The optional fields, such as the episode number, chapters and alternate files, are in tabs below the shownotes when the Field Group module is installed.*

## Complete the episode

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Label** | Episode title. Required. |
| **Podcast** | The show this episode belongs to. Required. |
| **Audio File** | The episode recording. Required; the form accepts `mp3`, `m4a` and `aac` audio, and `mp4`, `m4v` and `mov` video. |
| **Type** | **Full**, **Trailer**, or **Bonus**. Required; **Full** is selected by default. |
| **Episode Number** | Optional number; the form suggests the next number when opened from a podcast. |

You can also pick or upload an episode **Image** from the Media Library, enter comma-separated **Keywords**, write **Shownotes**, mark the episode **Explicit**, and add feed **Author** references. Drupal calculates the audio **Duration** when the episode is saved.

Podcast apps group episodes by **Season**. A **Season name** is optional; some apps show it instead of the number. List the hosts and guests of the episode under **People**. Apps show them instead of the podcast's people. Starting from a podcast's episode list fills in the podcast's people, so you only add guests.

Upload **Transcripts** as `vtt`, `srt` or `txt` files. After uploading, select a **Language** for each transcript that is not in the podcast's language. Apple Podcasts and other apps show VTT and SRT transcripts; only VTT can name the speaker of each line.

Add **Chapters** so listeners can jump within the episode. Each chapter has a **Start**, such as `1:02:03` or `62:03.5`, a **Title**, and an optional **Link** and **Image**. If the field is empty when you upload an MP3 or M4A file, the chapters stored in the file are imported. Apps that support chapters show them in the player.

Upload **Alternate files** for other versions of the episode, such as a lower bitrate, Opus or a video. Apps that support alternate enclosures let listeners choose one. The file's description becomes its title in the app, so describe it briefly, such as `Video` or `Low bandwidth`.

A published episode of type **Trailer** is also the podcast's trailer. If it has a season, it is the trailer of that season.

The allowed upload size shown on the form depends on your Drupal and server configuration. There is no fixed module-wide 100 MB limit to rely on.

An episode that moved here from another host keeps its old **Episode GUID**, so apps don't show it again as new. See [Episode GUIDs](/site-building/feed#episode-guids).

Leave **Published** checked to include the episode in a published podcast's feed. Uncheck it to keep the episode out of the feed while you prepare it. Click **Save** when ready.

## Give the episode its own page

The episode entity has a Drupal page, but the feed's item link is intended to point to a linked **Podcast Episode Page** node. [Create an episode page](/get-started/episode-page) if you want a dedicated public page for shownotes or other site content. Without a linked page, the current release's feed item link falls back to the site homepage.
